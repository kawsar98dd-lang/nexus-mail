User Guide: How to Operate

Global GST/VAT Master is designed for freelancers, business owners, and consumers to calculate precise tax liabilities. Follow these steps:

  • Step 1: Select Jurisdiction
    Choose your country from the dropdown list. The tool automatically loads standard VAT/GST rates for regions like USA, UK, India, Bangladesh, etc.
  • Step 2: Choose Tax Mode
    Use the toggle switch. Select Exclusive to add tax on top of a price, or Inclusive to extract tax from a total price (Reverse GST).
  • Step 3: Generate Invoice
    After calculation, click "Generate Invoice" to create a professional PDF receipt ready for download or printing.

Technical Algorithm: Inclusive vs Exclusive

Understanding the math behind the tax is crucial for financial accuracy. Here is how our Commercial Engine processes your numbers:

1. Exclusive Tax (Adding Tax)

Used when pricing products before tax. The formula is straightforward:

Total = Base Price + (Base Price × Rate %)

2. Inclusive Tax (Reverse Calculation)

Used when you have the final receipt price and need to find the original cost. This uses a reverse-coefficient formula:

Tax Amount = Total Price - (Total Price ÷ (1 + Rate / 100))
